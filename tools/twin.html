The twin: how the real fixture moves

pantiltengine now

“Apply” writes the twin's range and speed into the fixture's library file (the engine's model of it) and keeps your set's home pointing at the same DMX value. Restart the show afterwards.

Calibrate against the real fixture

  1. Range. Hold, then set tilt to 90 (or pan to 180). Change the twin's range until the real head points exactly level (or exactly backwards). The DMX follows the range, so the head moves as you edit.
  2. Speed. Marker: the head goes back and forth, lit only when the twin says it has stopped. A light streak while moving: the twin is too fast. Dark after stopping: too slow. Tune top speed with big sizes (180°), acceleration and braking with small ones (20–40°), the delay last.
  3. Check. Swing on beat at your usual tempo: does the real head turn back early, like the twin?
